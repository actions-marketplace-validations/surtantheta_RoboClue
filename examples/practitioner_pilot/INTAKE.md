# Before the trial

Reply with as much or as little as you can share:

1. What question are you trying to answer, and what decision depends on it?
2. Which stack and native artifact formats do you use? What is your current command, viewer, script or workaround?
3. Is this a real incident, healthy control, unresolved question or demo? Has its answer already been disclosed to the operator?
4. Can the question be answered from saved artifacts? Which parts require runtime, simulator, calibration or robot evidence we do not have?
5. Do you have authorized small inputs, a correction/control and a later input? Missing items are acceptable. Never upload credentials or private robot/customer data.
6. What would make a retained result useful to a colleague? Who, if anyone, can check it independently?

Participation does not imply permission to name you, quote you, publish artifacts or post an upstream issue. We will ask separately for each intended publication. You can request deletion of unpublished submitted materials. Internal AI tests are recorded separately and do not count as practitioner participation.
