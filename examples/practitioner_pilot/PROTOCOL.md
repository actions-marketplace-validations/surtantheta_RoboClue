# Practitioner pilot procedure

Recruitment target: three independent practitioners and five independently supplied real incident/healthy pairs, at least two outside LeRobot. At least four cases must be prospectively answerable in the claimed scope; retain unsupported and missing-evidence attempts outside that answerable denominator. Do not silently substitute known demos. These are targets, not observations.

Start with a short fit check. A useful negative outcome is “my recurring problem is unsupported” with the smallest missing capability identified. Do not add a framework adapter to rescue the score during a frozen comparison.

Before diagnosis, the participant/custodian records the question, decision, native artifacts, allowed evidence, reference authority, intended invariant, expected endpoint and answerability. A separate custodian holds the correctness evidence if an unfamiliar-case comparison is possible. Freeze the package, baseline and instructions; preserve every attempt. Known-answer examples are onboarding controls.

The participant's competent usual workflow is the baseline. Allow existing upstream fixes, documentation, viewers, native APIs and maintained scripts; a plain file diff is not automatically the baseline. Give both routes the same original evidence and setup opportunities. Charge new scripts, exports, contract selection and data reduction to the route requiring them. Record supplied helpers and their availability/maintenance separately from recurring work.

Use matched unfamiliar cases with counterbalanced route assignment or independent matched operators. With one person repeating the same case, record route order, answer exposure and learning effects; do not make an unbiased time-saving claim. Internal agent runs are a separate engineering screen. A participant watching an agent is not automatically a human comparative trial.

Measure installation, preparation, diagnosis, correction acquisition/verification, handoff, colleague reproduction and later reuse separately. Report hands-on time, machine/elapsed time, interruptions, new code, mistakes and abandonment. Never substitute tool-call counts, report size or storage compression for human work saved. A 20-minute fit check does not meet the whole-job endpoint unless the whole job actually completes.

The whole-job endpoint is a supported answer with traceable evidence, a supplied correction checked when one exists, a retained result a colleague can independently verify after relocation, and an unchanged criterion tested on a later original input. Absence of a correction must remain absence; do not invent physical repair. Where an input/format cannot support this endpoint, record the gap.

A reviewer adjudicates each route against evidence other than the tool's own status. Distinguish artifact agreement, process/runtime observations, causal explanation and physical/policy competence. Check healthy/unknown cases for false fault or false pass. Reproducing a claim is different from proving it true or showing successful motion.

The existing engineering go criterion is at least 50% fewer differential manual operations across a complete matched job, with preparation and reuse charged fairly and correctness preserved. Report operation definitions and raw counts prospectively. Human time, stated reuse intent, actual voluntary reuse and issue/fix retention are separate outcomes; none follows from that engineering threshold. A 10× benefit and broader adoption require separate evidence.

Check in once after seven days, if the participant agrees: did they voluntarily use it on another project input, retain a check in a real workflow, or abandon it? No reply is missing follow-up, not non-use or adoption. Record retention of a command/report separately from a verified portable case. Do not offer stars as a success metric or ask participants to endorse an untested claim.

Before broader launch, publish the bounded result and limitations with participant permission. A small successful pilot cannot establish universal compatibility, exclusivity or community-wide demand. If competent native workflows are equally easy, retain the null result and prioritize the missing recurring capability rather than adding report polish.
