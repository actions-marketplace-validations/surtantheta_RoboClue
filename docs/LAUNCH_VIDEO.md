The demonstration plays above as an animated preview. Download the
[HD square cut](../assets/launch/roboclue-square.mp4?raw=1),
[45-second full cut](../assets/launch/roboclue-full.mp4?raw=1) or
[12-second terminal loop](../assets/launch/roboclue-terminal-loop.mp4?raw=1).

The opening uses 1920×1080 real-robot footage from the NVIDIA-coauthored
DexMimicGen project. Its complete camera width is retained so the robot's head,
both hands and placement trays remain visible. A small credit discloses the
adjusted 6× playback. This is robotics context, not a RoboClue-controlled
rollout or evidence of a physical-robot improvement.
[Authors, source, modifications and CC-BY-SA4 license](../assets/launch/ROBOT-FOOTAGE-CREDIT.md).

The five-second opening gives each line its own glow reveal. “One wrong setting”
reveals from 0.10 to 1.65 seconds. After a quarter-second pause, “Training can
still run” reveals from 1.90 to 3.45 seconds. Both stay visible until the cut
at 5 seconds. The robot footage is slowed to span that same five-second interval.
Main opening fonts retain the previously approved smaller sizes.
The full cut's later benefit frame says “Find what's
wrong” and “See what to change”, rather than listing unexplained categories.

The CLI sequence is a real captured configuration check. It shows an observed
action-chunk setting of20, a pinned published setting of10, the source and a
suggested correction. The footage is unrelated to that example; no robot-score
change is claimed. The [raw stdout](../release/0.1.0/recipe.stdout.txt) is retained.
Only wrapping and visual emphasis change the displayed excerpt.

The [aesthetics review](../release/0.1.0/AESTHETICS-REVIEW.md) and
[VC-style editorial review](../release/0.1.0/VIDEO-REVIEW.md) are separate AI
assessments, not human audience studies or investor endorsements. Technical
[video validation](../release/0.1.0/FINAL-VALIDATION.json) binds the exact files.
Detailed native training proofs remain in the [evidence guide](EVIDENCE.md).
The end card contains only RoboClue and the repository URL.
